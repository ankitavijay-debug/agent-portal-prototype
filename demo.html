Acme Service: personal agents over Poppy, humans that decide

Priya's personal agent (Claude, ChatGPT, MUSE or any Poppy-compatible agent) reaches Acme through the open Personal Agent Protocol (Poppy). Acme's portal agent and background agents do the work. People approve what matters.

The story

    • Agents handle reads and reversible steps on their own.
    • Humans decide money, account changes and policy exceptions.
    • The portal is the only place those decisions are made, so no agent can approve itself.
    • Poppy is the open standard any personal agent (Claude, ChatGPT, MUSE, others) uses to reach Acme, so Acme builds one front door, not one per agent: what Acme offers, what it excludes, who the agent and user are. It does not decide approvals. That is Acme's layer on top.
